Project title:
QuantumGenome EDS Discovery Engine

Project description:
Built a real-data genomics discovery pipeline that mapped EDS/collagen variant interaction structure from ClinVar and 1000 Genomes, validated low-energy QAOA disease-load patterns in PLOD1 and B3GALT6, and expanded into a Jetson-first local system for same-gene, same-disease, position-window, mixed-biologic, novel-candidate, and epistasis-hypothesis discovery.
